# SEO phase 2 evidence and decisions — 28 August 2026

## Evidence boundary

- Google Search Console was deliberately skipped because the site is new and the owner said there is no useful query history yet.
- The keyword snapshot came from Semrush's United Kingdom database. Volumes are estimated UK demand, not Coventry enquiries, traffic, rankings, or conversions.
- Blank metrics mean unavailable or unreported. Only `burglary repair locksmith` returned an explicit measured zero in this batch.
- The bounded connector snapshot is preserved in `2026-08-28-locksmith-keyword-batch-uk.csv`.
- After one related-keyword report, the Semrush API balance reached zero units. Further connector requests returned error 132. Research continued read-only in the existing signed-in Microsoft Edge session.
- Semrush's domain organic reports returned `NOTHING FOUND`. That is unavailable ranking evidence, not a measured rank of zero.

## Topic architecture changes

- Article-level calls to action now override broad pillar defaults for burglary, lost-key, moving-house, broken-key, and lockout topics.
- A single service-to-guide map is reused by the five service pages and all 35 hand-written town-service pages.
- Area hubs now choose four guides from their described housing stock and common lock issues instead of rotating unrelated posts by array position.
- The legacy security, emergency, legal, seasonal, uPVC, cost, and Coventry article sets were reviewed for unsupported statistics, fixed attack-time claims, universal insurance language, and unverified guarantees. Materially changed articles now use qualified wording and primary-source links where an authoritative source applies.

## Backlink and authority snapshot

Semrush reported Authority Score 2, 70 backlinks, 44 referring domains, 31 follow links, 37 nofollow links, 0 sponsored links, 1 UGC link, and 60 linked URLs. These metrics are a dated tool snapshot, not a quality verdict.

The visible referring-domain list included established directories and many low-authority, spam-looking domains. No source-URL, anchor, ownership, manual-action, or submitted-disavow evidence justified a disavow. No backlink classifications or external profiles were changed.

## What this work does not prove

The patch improves keyword ownership, internal linking, content accuracy, accessibility, and crawlable page structure. It does not prove Google discovery, indexation, rankings, local-pack visibility, calls, or enquiries. Those need later live and search evidence after deployment.
