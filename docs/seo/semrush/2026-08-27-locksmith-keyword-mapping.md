## Evidence boundary

- Source: authenticated Semrush UI in Microsoft Edge, United Kingdom database, Bulk Keyword Analysis.
- Volume is Semrush's estimated average monthly UK search volume. It is not a count of local enquiries and does not prove that searches came from a named town.
- A blank metric is unavailable or unreported, not zero. Rows showing `0` are retained as explicit measured zeroes.

## Measured local demand and canonical ownership

| locksmith kenilworth | 140 | `/areas/kenilworth` |

The five dedicated service pages own Coventry service intent. The 35 existing hand-written town-service pages own service intent for Nuneaton, Bedworth, Rugby, Leamington Spa, Warwick, Kenilworth, and Stratford-upon-Avon. For every other place, the existing area hub remains the canonical owner and the service-area URL continues to redirect there.

Exact town-service queries were frequently blank/unreported or low-volume in this batch. The implementation therefore localises measured UK service wording inside the existing canonical pages; it does not generate new service-area URLs or treat missing metrics as zero.

## Exclusions

Automotive keyword expansion, car keys, key cutting, safe opening, locksmith training, and unrelated product-shopping terms were excluded because the repository does not establish dedicated pages and supporting service detail for those intents.
