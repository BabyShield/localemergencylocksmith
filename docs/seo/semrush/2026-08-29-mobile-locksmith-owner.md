## Ownership decision

The homepage at `/` is the canonical owner for broad Coventry locksmith intent, including `mobile locksmith`. A separate mobile-locksmith URL would duplicate the same attendance model, services and location coverage without evidence for a distinct page.

The homepage now explains the useful meaning of the term: attendance at the affected property after the full address, exact entrance, current availability, ETA and price basis are confirmed. It does not turn “mobile” into a claim about current proximity, van stock, guaranteed attendance or a fixed journey time.

The release crawler also protects the visible core intent for the homepage, prices page and all five canonical service pages so later copy edits cannot silently remove the measured query families from their assigned owners.
